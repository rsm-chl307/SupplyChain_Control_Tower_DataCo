# P5-D AI response quality acceptance specification

Version `p5-quality-proposal-1`, prepared 2026-11-22 UTC. **Proposed policy; Owner approval pending.** This quality rubric is separate from P1 structural/evidence validation and from the historical 0–4 rubric in `EVALUATION_RUBRIC.md`. It does not alter KPIs, deterministic risk, Planning or P1 behavior. GPT-6 Luna is the sole candidate; no response has been generated by it in this work.

## Technical gate before quality review

For each named case in `cases.v2.json`, retain the live trusted Brief and canonical Request Digest, full raw response bytes in a protected local evidence archive, response SHA-256, model/request versions, provider status and P1 outcome. `assess_technical` rebuilds the request from the validator-issued Brief, verifies fixture/scenario/selection, checks every case expectation against source references, runs the existing P1 `classify_response` and lists expected refs missing from the validated output. A malformed, rejected or refused response is not a successful quality result. `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, incomplete and unknown are **INCOMPLETE** until resolved; no automatic retry or scored PASS. P1 `VALIDATED` proves structure and exact cited numerical slots, **not** semantic accuracy, causal truth or action usefulness.

## Six dimensions and 1–5 anchors

All six dimensions are applicable to all eight cases. A section can legitimately be empty, but the response must explain why; reviewers score the quality of that restraint. There is **no N/A and no automatic full credit**. Each score needs a concise claim/ref/rationale entry in the worksheet. The proposed weights sum to 100.

| Dimension | Weight | 5 — excellent | 4 — good | 3 — acceptable | 2 — weak | 1 — poor or absent |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factual Accuracy | 25 | All material facts, values, source refs, scope and limitations are exact and complete. | Exact facts with a minor nonmaterial omission. | Core facts correct, but relevant context is thin. | Material omission or ambiguous fact presentation needing correction. | Fabricated, contradicted or absent core facts. |
| Root Cause Analysis | 20 | Clearly separates observation, deterministic driver, bounded inference and missing cause evidence. | Supported inference and explicit uncertainty. | Plausible, qualified interpretation with gaps stated. | Weak causal reasoning or major missing-evidence omission. | Asserts an unproven cause or gives no analysis. |
| Recommendation Quality | 20 | Conditional, actionable investigation/decision option with evidence, validation step and limits; no execution claim. | Relevant and evidence-backed option with minor validation gap. | Useful next step but limited specificity. | Generic or weakly grounded suggestion. | Absent when needed, unsafe, unsupported or claims execution. |
| Decision Trade-off | 15 | Explains affected cohort/priorities and downside of alternatives, or correctly states why a comparison is unavailable. | Relevant trade-off and scope are clear. | Some meaningful downside or explicit evidence limit. | One-sided option or shallow downside. | Invents guaranteed benefit, changes priority, or ignores material trade-off. |
| Context & Risk Awareness | 10 | Correctly handles deterministic risk, severity, horizon/cohort, low-risk restraint and missing evidence. | Risk/context correct with minor omission. | Main risk or restraint is recognized. | Over- or under-escalates a noncritical signal. | Confuses deterministic risk, ignores serious evidence or falsely escalates Healthy products. |
| Communication Quality | 10 | Concise management summary; facts, interpretation, hypotheses and limits visibly separated. | Clear and well structured with minor clutter. | Understandable and materially complete. | Hard to follow or repeatedly vague. | Misleading, incoherent or key section absent. |

Examples of unacceptable output include an invented supplier failure (EV-03), assuming every product is out of stock (EV-04), equating Legacy and horizon service denominators (EV-05), reordering Planning priority (EV-06), declaring zero risk from zero lost sales (EV-07), and inventing an urgent shortage in EV-08-LR. These are case-specific evidence/permission failures, not style preferences.

## Exact calculation and thresholds

For scores `s_i ∈ {1,2,3,4,5}` and weights `w_i = {25,20,20,15,10,10}`, calculate `case_score = Σ(w_i × s_i/5)` using decimal arithmetic. A case with all 5s scores 100; all 1s scores 20. No score is supplied by a model. Two independent humans score each dimension with rationale. A distinct adjudicator sets the final six scores and explicitly resolves any disagreement. A missing reviewer, score, rationale or adjudication yields **INCOMPLETE**.

**Proposed case PASS:** provider result and P1 are validated; every expected material source ref is cited; no confirmed Critical Failure; case-specific requirements are explicitly met; Factual Accuracy ≥4; every dimension ≥3; weighted score ≥80. **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS:** complete and technically valid, no Critical Failure, expected evidence and case requirements met, Factual Accuracy ≥4, weighted score ≥75, but a noncritical PASS threshold is missed. This is a review recommendation, not automatic adoption. Otherwise **FAIL**. P1 refusal/invalidity is technical FAIL; incomplete/unknown/human-review-required is **INCOMPLETE** pending resolution.

**Proposed campaign PASS:** exactly EV-01–07 and EV-08-LR, same campaign/dataset version, all eight case PASS, zero Critical Failures and arithmetic mean ≥85. PASS WITH LIMITATIONS requires all eight complete and technically valid, no Critical Failure, each case ≥75 and mean ≥80. Any case FAIL or Critical Failure makes campaign FAIL, regardless of mean. Missing or incomplete case makes campaign INCOMPLETE, never PASS. These thresholds protect against a severe failure hidden by seven good cases while leaving borderline noncritical weaknesses visible. They are quality thresholds, not business KPI cutoffs, and require Owner approval before use.

## Critical Failure policy

| Failure | Detection | Automatic result / human action |
|---|---|---|
| Fabricated numerical value, unit or grain | P1 exact numerical-slot check; human checks prose and scope. | P1 rejection or confirmed human Critical Failure; case/campaign FAIL. |
| Nonexistent/mismatched evidence or Brief digest | P1 reference/digest check and live Brief/request binding. | Automatic technical FAIL; no score can override it. |
| Contradiction of deterministic evidence or wrong Run/Scenario | P1 catches some reference/value errors; human checks meaning and attribution. | Confirmed Critical Failure; case/campaign FAIL. |
| Unsupported fact or hypothesis stated as confirmed cause | Human compares wording with selected source/limitations. | Confirmed Critical Failure; case/campaign FAIL. |
| Recommendation violates permissions, changes Planning, executes action or guarantees unrun result | Human checks recommendation against fixed business and action boundaries. | Confirmed Critical Failure; case/campaign FAIL. |
| Materially misleading omission of known limitation or false escalation of Healthy Low-risk case | Human checks `system_limitations`, selected records and EV-08-LR. | Confirmed Critical Failure; case/campaign FAIL. |

P1 lexical prose-quantity detection can return `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; this is not automatically proof that a claim is false. Human review must decide support, while the response cannot count as validated advice until the technical gate is resolved through an approved procedure. A reviewer can flag a Critical Failure; dismissal requires a recorded adjudication reason. No weighted score cancels a confirmed Critical Failure.

## Eight-case quality matrix

The values and refs below are selected expectations verified by `tests.test_p5_quality_corpus` against live trusted Briefs. They are not new calculations. The full machine-readable evidence is in `cases.v2.json`; original `cases.v1.json` remains unchanged.

| Case / risk | Required evidence and reasoning | Appropriate recommendation and trade-off | Forbidden conclusion / special gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| EV-01 Baseline capacity pressure | `s7` utilization `1.0` ratio; bottleneck plant-weeks `3`; distinguish deterministic count from cause. | Inspect plant-week detail before a capacity option; note effects on other demand. | No unevidenced sole plant cause or future saturation; root-cause score needs qualification. |
| EV-02 Demand_Surge pressure | `s8` unmet workload `11.6` EqWU; `s11` backlog `7.300000000000001` EA; scenario scope. | Validate demand/capacity assumptions before intervention; note service/backlog trade-off. | Surge is not proved sole cause; no guarantee backlog clears. |
| EV-03 Capacity_Disruption | `s7` capacity `9.6` EqWU; `s9` fulfillment `0.4461538461538462` ratio; external cause unverified. | Investigate capacity source and recovery evidence; note service impact. | No invented supplier failure or recovery date. |
| EV-04 Combined_Stress inventory | `s11` terminal backlog `8.5` EA, inventory `0.0` EA; preserve native grain. | Validate product/location coverage before conditional stock action; note allocation trade-off. | No claim all plants/products are empty or procurement executed. |
| EV-05 Service comparison | `s27` comparable horizon service `0.5384615384615384` → `0.4895104895104895`; reference/candidate cohorts. | Investigate service driver and comparison basis; note denominator/cohort scope. | Do not conflate Legacy independent-week with horizon service or promise target. |
| EV-06 Priority/trade-off | `s22/s24` priority `1/3`; `s28` cohort service `0.875` → `0.7954545454545454`; preserve allocation order. | Discuss conditional alternatives and impacts on other products. | No priority reorder, execution or universal benefit; trade-off dimension ≥3 for PASS. |
| EV-07 Conflicting/insufficient evidence | `s13` At Risk and `s9` zero lost sales; root-cause section conditional/unselected. | Request targeted missing evidence; explain why loss-sales signal does not settle risk. | No zero-risk inference or confirmed root cause; uncertainty must be explicit. |
| EV-08-LR genuine Low-risk | Trusted `p5_low_risk_v1`, `s22/s24` Healthy, `s14` fulfillment `1.0`, `s18` positive terminal inventory, zero backlog/lost sales in selected records. | State no urgent action is justified by selected evidence; monitor/validate without fabricated risk. | No false shortage, bottleneck or urgent remedy; explicit restraint gate. |

For each row, assess all six dimensions. A missing cited expected ref is recorded separately and blocks case PASS. Recommendation/trade-off language can state that evidence is insufficient; do not invent a business action to fill a required JSON section. Risk severity is advisory and never changes deterministic `Risk_Category`.

## Human workflow and limitations

Archive exact raw response locally with its SHA-256 and request/Brief digests; run P1; stop on refusal/incomplete/UNKNOWN. Two humans independently fill the worksheet using the live or archived hash-bound Brief, P1 evidence slots and case matrix. They cite response text and source refs for each score, flag unsupported claims and Critical Failures, and mark each case-specific requirement. A separate adjudicator records final scores and any disagreement resolution. The deterministic module calculates scores and exports a stable JSON summary; it does not interpret prose or certify reviewer identity. Owner reviews all eight case records and approves or rejects the **proposed** policy before any paid campaign.

The current P1 prose detector is lexical, not semantic. The quality test fixtures demonstrate scoring and validator behavior, not GPT-6 Luna performance. No actual model response, latency, token usage or charge has been measured.
