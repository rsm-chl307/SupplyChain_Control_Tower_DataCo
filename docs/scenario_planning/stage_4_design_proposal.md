# Stage 4 — Decision / Allocation Engine Design and Implementation Record

## Approved business-rule freeze

Stage 4 consumes only the Stage 3 `scenario_snapshot.csv` contract. The allocation grain is `Scenario × Plant × Product × Week`; capacity is shared at `Scenario × Plant × Week`. The existing Plant × Product rows are the complete eligibility set. Cross-plant transfers are not supported in the MVP.

The MVP allocates independently by week. Backlog does not carry into later weeks. `Beginning_Inventory` is product-specific and plant-specific and is consumed only within the same Plant × Product × Week × Scenario record. `Backlog` is the single same-week unmet-demand field.

Frozen formulas:

```text
Net_Demand = max(Scenario_Demand - Beginning_Inventory, 0)
Allocated_Qty = min(Net_Demand, Remaining_Capacity)
Backlog = Net_Demand - Allocated_Qty
Remaining_Capacity = Remaining_Capacity - Allocated_Qty
```

Products are ordered by:

```text
Planning_Priority DESC
Net_Demand DESC
Product Card Id ASC
```

Baseline, Demand Surge, and Capacity Disruption use the same scenario-agnostic engine. `Scenario_Capacity` is the allocation pool; `Baseline_Available_Capacity` is context only. Inconsistent Scenario_Capacity values within a Scenario × Plant × Week group are hard failures.

Stage 4 excludes KPI calculation, Service Level, Utilization, risk scores, recommendations, AI/RAG, forecasting, optimization, dashboard/UI work, and Stage 5 functionality.

## Architecture

- `src/allocation_engine.py` — in-memory allocation for one Scenario × Plant × Week group.
- `src/allocation_manager.py` — in-memory orchestration across all groups.
- `src/allocation_validator.py` — input/output business-rule quality gate.
- `tests/test_allocation_engine.py` — focused allocation and validation coverage.

No core module reads or writes CSV files. The output artifact is generated by an external orchestration step from `data/processed/scenario_snapshot.csv`.

## Output contract

The allocation result preserves the Stage 3 fields and adds:

- `Net_Demand`
- `Allocated_Qty`
- `Backlog`
- `Remaining_Capacity`
- `Allocation_Order`
- `Allocation_Strategy`
- `Allocation_Reason`

Stage 5 will consume these results for performance evaluation.
